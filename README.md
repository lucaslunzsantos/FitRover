# FitRover
Get healthier and stronger now! Available for Android only.

## What is FitRover?
FitRover is an application designed for individuals seeking to improve their health, build strength, or maintain their weight. The process is straightforward, akin to unboxing a new coffee machine: you create a new account using your email address, password, and username, or you can also log in if you already have an account.

## What will I see when I first open the app?
FitRover is designed to be simple at first glance. It is familiar, just like Google Fit, Samsung Health, or Strava. It runs smoothly, and it works fine with your smartwatch.

## Is there any FitRover edition for iPhone?
Since the iOS platform is more expensive than the Google Play Console signature, we do not have plans to launch for Apple's ecosystem. Maybe next time in the future.
